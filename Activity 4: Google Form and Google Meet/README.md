Through this activity, I learned how Google Meet and Google Forms can make communication, collaboration, and information gathering easier and more organized. In the future, I can apply these skills in my academic activities, group projects, and nursing practice. I can continue improving my digital skills by exploring more features and using these tools responsibly and effectively in different learning and professional situations.
