ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION °❀.ೃ࿔

This activity focused on understanding how colors and typography influence the overall appearance, identity, and message of a design. I explored the process of selecting and combining colors to establish a consistent visual theme, as well as choosing fonts that complement one another and improve readability. For my output, I used a soft pastel-inspired color palette, including Pastel Crimson (#F39EB6), Light Yellow (#F7F6D3), Light Red (#FFE5E0), and Light Yellow (#FFFDF1). I also incorporated Amsterdam Four, Poppins Bold, and Petrona to create a distinctive visual identity. Through this activity, I learned how intentional design choices can communicate a particular mood and personality even before someone reads the actual content.

WHAT I HAVE LEARNED °❀.ೃ࿔

I learned that colors and fonts are not merely decorative elements; they play an important role in how people perceive and understand a design. Different colors can communicate different emotions, while typography can establish the personality and tone of a project. I also learned that choosing attractive colors and fonts is not enough. They must work together harmoniously, maintain readability, and support the purpose of the design.

One challenge I encountered was selecting colors and typefaces that complemented one another without making the design look inconsistent or overwhelming. I addressed this by experimenting with different combinations and assigning specific roles to each font and color. This helped me establish a more cohesive visual identity while maintaining a balance between creativity and readability.

In the future, I can apply this knowledge when creating posters, presentations, infographics, social media materials, and personal or professional branding projects. As a nursing student, I can also use these principles when developing health education materials, ensuring that important information is presented in a visually organized and accessible manner.
