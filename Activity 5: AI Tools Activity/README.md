# ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION °❀.ೃ࿔ 

This activity involved using AI-assisted planning and documentation to develop an event proposal for REIGNITE: Rekindling the Fire Within, a youth night for the Lord of the Harvest Christian Community. The project focused on organizing the different elements needed to conduct a successful youth gathering, including the event rationale, objectives, proposed program flow, activities, committee assignments, budget, preparation timeline, and evaluation plan. I also developed an event preparation checklist to identify the important arrangements that needed to be completed before the gathering. Through this project, I explored how technology and AI can assist in transforming an initial event concept into a structured and organized proposal. 


# WHAT I HAVE LEARNED °❀.ೃ࿔

This activity taught me that organizing an event requires more than coming up with a theme or selecting activities. It involves proper planning, coordination, delegation, budgeting, time management, and communication among the people involved. I learned how to break down a large event into smaller responsibilities, establish a realistic program flow, identify the resources needed, and prepare an organized proposal. I also gained experience using AI as a planning assistant to structure ideas and improve the presentation of formal documents.

One challenge I encountered was ensuring that all parts of the proposal were connected and realistic. The program schedule, committee assignments, proposed budget, and preparation timeline needed to complement one another. I addressed this by organizing the event requirements systematically, reviewing the proposed arrangements, and making adjustments to ensure that the plans were practical and consistent. This helped me understand that AI can assist with planning, but the final decisions still require human judgment and consideration of actual circumstances.

I can apply these skills in future school activities, organizational responsibilities, community programs, and even healthcare-related events such as health education seminars and nursing conferences. As a future nurse, I recognize that planning and coordination are also important in delivering organized patient care and working effectively with a healthcare team. This activity encouraged me to become more systematic, responsible, and prepared when handling tasks that involve multiple people and responsibilities.
