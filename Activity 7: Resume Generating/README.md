# ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION °❀.ೃ࿔ 

This activity focused on exploring how different artificial intelligence platforms can assist in creating professional documents, particularly resumes. I provided the same personal information, educational background, clinical experiences, skills, and instructions to different AI platforms to generate and compare their resume outputs. The purpose was to evaluate how each platform interpreted the given prompts and presented professional qualifications through its own formatting, structure, and design. After reviewing the generated resumes, I selected the Gemini-generated version as my preferred final output because I found its layout more organized, readable, and appropriate for a nursing job application. # WHAT I HAVE LEARNED °❀.ೃ࿔

Through this activity, I learned that artificial intelligence can make professional document preparation more convenient and efficient. I gained experience in writing specific prompts, organizing personal information, and evaluating the quality of AI-generated content. I also discovered that different AI platforms can produce different results even when given identical instructions. This helped me understand the importance of comparing outputs instead of automatically accepting the first result generated by an AI tool.

One challenge I encountered was determining which resume was more suitable for a professional nursing application. Both platforms were able to organize my qualifications and experiences, but their approaches to formatting, spacing, and content presentation differed. I addressed this by comparing their readability, organization, professional appearance, and how effectively they highlighted my clinical experiences and extracurricular involvement. I ultimately chose the Gemini-generated resume while recognizing that it still required personal review and necessary adjustments to ensure that all information accurately represented my qualifications.

In the future, I can apply these skills when preparing professional documents, application letters, portfolios, and other career-related materials. As I prepare for future nursing opportunities, I understand the importance of presenting my qualifications accurately and professionally. This activity also reminded me that AI should serve as an assistant rather than a replacement for personal judgment, since I remain responsible for the accuracy and authenticity of everything I submit.
