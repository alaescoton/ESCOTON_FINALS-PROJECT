# ⋆. 𐙚˚࿔ SKILLS AND TOOLS I HAVE LEARNED 𝜗𝜚˚⋆
Throughout GE 4120: 21st Century IT Skills, I gained valuable knowledge and developed a wider range of digital, technical, and creative skills that I can apply both academically and personally. One of the most important skills I developed was effective AI prompting, which taught me how to communicate my ideas clearly, provide specific instructions, and refine AI-generated outputs according to my intended results. I became more familiar with using artificial intelligence tools such as ChatGPT and Gemini for brainstorming, writing, organizing information, developing content, and creating professional documents. I also explored Enter.Coverage AI for website development, which allowed me to transform a simple business idea into an actual online platform without requiring advanced coding knowledge.

Aside from using AI tools, I strengthened my skills in graphic design, digital branding, visual communication, and content creation. I learned how to establish a consistent visual identity by selecting appropriate color palettes, typography, layouts, and design elements that communicate the purpose and personality of a project. I also gained experience in organizing information, conducting online research, evaluating the reliability of sources, and presenting ideas in a clear and understandable manner. Another important skill I developed was the ability to critically assess AI-generated content rather than immediately accepting its results. Through comparing outputs from different AI platforms, I learned that effective technology use requires proper instructions, fact-checking, personal judgment, and necessary revisions.

I was able to apply these skills through several projects and activities throughout the course. These included developing MEDSAFE, a medication safety and verification system concept that addresses misinformation and unverified online sources involving Ozempic and tirzepatide. This project allowed me to combine my nursing background with technology by conceptualizing features such as a medication information hub, product and source verification guide, social media claim checker, and healthcare consultation referral. I also explored personal branding through Ashley's Sweet Little Moments, created promotional and advertising materials, and developed a pastel-themed online journal shop featuring different journal collections, product customization, and shopping features. Additionally, I used multiple AI platforms to generate and compare professional resumes, evaluating their organization, readability, formatting, and overall presentation before selecting the output that best suited my intended professional application. I also applied my skills in planning and organizing by developing an event proposal for REIGNITE: Youth Night 2026, which included its concept paper, program flow, committee responsibilities, proposed budget, and event preparation plans.

# ⋆. 𐙚˚࿔ MY LEARNING EXPERIENCE 𝜗𝜚˚⋆
My experience in GE 4120 taught me that technology is more than just a tool for completing academic requirements. It is a powerful resource that allows people to express creativity, develop ideas, address real-world concerns, and find practical solutions to everyday challenges. Before taking this course, I had limited experience in creating websites and using AI tools for more complex projects. However, through the different laboratory activities, I became more confident in exploring unfamiliar platforms and discovering how they could be used to accomplish tasks that I once thought required advanced technical knowledge.

What made this course particularly meaningful was learning how to connect technology with my personal interests and future profession. As a nursing student, developing MEDSAFE helped me recognize how digital innovation can contribute to medication safety and health education, while my online journal shop allowed me to explore my creativity and turn a personal interest into a potential business concept. I also realized that although AI can make tasks faster and more convenient, it cannot replace human creativity, critical thinking, responsibility, and decision-making. Its outputs still require careful evaluation to ensure that the information is accurate, appropriate, and aligned with the intended purpose.

Overall, GE 4120 has broadened my understanding of what I can accomplish through technology. It strengthened not only my digital and technical abilities but also my confidence, creativity, problem-solving skills, and willingness to learn beyond my usual field of study. Most importantly, it taught me that being a future healthcare professional also means being open to innovation and continuous learning, especially in a world where technology is becoming an increasingly important part of education, healthcare, and everyday life.
