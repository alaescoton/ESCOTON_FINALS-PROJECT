# ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION °❀.ೃ࿔ 

This activity involved developing an online journal shop using Enter.Coverage AI, allowing me to transform a personal interest in journaling into a digital business concept. The website was designed to showcase different journal collections, including Classic, Leather, and Fluffy journals, with prices ranging from ₱200 to ₱350. It also included pastel color options, product descriptions, customization features, shopping cart functions, and an ordering section. Another important feature was the "All About the Author" section, where I shared my personal inspiration for creating the shop and explained how journaling became a meaningful way for me to express my thoughts and manage emotions. The main goal was to create an accessible, user-friendly, and visually appealing online platform that would encourage others to discover the value of journaling. 


# WHAT I HAVE LEARNED °❀.ೃ࿔

This activity helped me understand how website development works and how AI can assist in turning a business idea into an actual digital platform. I learned how to communicate specific website requirements through detailed prompts, including the layout, color scheme, product categories, descriptions, navigation, and desired features. I also gained a better understanding of user experience, website organization, product presentation, and the importance of making an online platform accessible and easy to navigate. Most importantly, I discovered that creating a website does not always require advanced coding knowledge when the appropriate tools and instructions are used.

One challenge I encountered was translating my ideas into specific instructions that the AI platform could understand and implement. I initially had to think carefully about the website's appearance, the features I wanted to include, and how customers would navigate the different sections. I addressed this by providing more detailed prompts, organizing the website requirements, and refining the generated output according to my preferred design. This experience taught me that the quality of an AI-generated website depends greatly on the clarity of the instructions and the user's willingness to review and improve the results.

What made this activity especially meaningful was the opportunity to connect technology with something personal to me. As a nursing student, journaling has become one of my ways of expressing emotions, organizing thoughts, and finding comfort amid academic and clinical responsibilities. Through this project, I realized that personal interests can become meaningful business ideas when combined with creativity and technology. In the future, I can apply these skills when developing digital platforms for healthcare education, patient information, or other entrepreneurial projects.
